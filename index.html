⭐ Loan of the Season · 2024/25
Joel
Colwill
AM Wales
Cheltenham Town → Exeter City
League Two → League One
46 appearances across two loan spells gave Colwill exactly what Cardiff needed him to have — physicality. The step up in tempo and contact at League Two and League One level hardened him considerably, and when he returned to Cardiff for 2025/26 he slotted into the senior squad with a comfort and authority that would simply not have been there without those seasons away.

LB Wales

Belgian Challenger Pro League
Playing week-in, week-out in Belgian football at a high level gave Bagan the consistency and competitive edge that has made him one of Cardiff's most dependable performers since his return. Rarely flashy, always reliable — he has become the kind of player Cardiff can build around on the left side, and that platform was built in Waregem.

⭐ Loan of the Season · 2022/23
Mark
McGuinness
CB Ireland
Sheffield Wednesday
EFL League One
McGuinness was so good at Sheffield Wednesday that Cardiff recalled him in January — and he immediately slotted straight into the first team, completing a full 90 minutes in every single Cardiff game he featured in for the rest of the 2022/23 season. That kind of seamless transition from loan to first team is exactly what the programme is designed to produce.

📋 Loan of the Season · 2020/21
Aden
Flint
CB England
Sheffield Wednesday
EFL Championship
Four appearances. 287 minutes. That's your loan of the season. Flint wasn't sent to Wednesday because anyone thought it was going to unlock something — he was shipped out to clear his wages off the books. He barely played. The whole season had that energy; a scrappy, poorly structured group of loans with no real thread running through them. One to look back on and learn from.

⭐ Loan of the Season · 2019/20
Bobby
DCR
AM Jamaica
Fulham
EFL Championship
Look, Bobby wasn't loan of the season because he tore it up for Cardiff — he was loaned to Fulham, played 46 games, helped them into the play-offs, and then Fulham bought him permanently. Cardiff got paid. Sometimes the best loan is the one that turns into a transfer fee, and in a season where the budget was tight, that money mattered.
